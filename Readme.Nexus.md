[center][size=5]Capacity & QuickBar Tweaks[/size][/center]

[b]A simple UE4SS mod for Subnautica 2 to customize storage capacity and quickbar slots.[/b]

[line]

[size=4][color=#f9a85d][b]Features[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Custom Storage Capacity:[/b] Adjust the number of slots and columns for each locker type individually.[/*]
[*][b]QuickBar Expansion:[/b] Increase your quickbar slots beyond the default count (1-10).[/*]
[*][b]Live Updates:[/b] Changes in the mod settings take effect immediately while in-game.[/*]
[*][b]Multi Language Support:[/b] The mod automatically detects your in-game language and translates the settings accordingly. If your language is not yet supported, it safely falls back to English. [/*]
[/list]
[size=4][color=#f9a85d][b]Requirements[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/subnautica2/mods/36]UE4SS - Subnautica 2[/url][/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/subnautica2/mods/20]Mod Settings for Subnautica 2[/url][/*]
[/list]
[size=4][color=#f9a85d][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Ensure [b]UE4SS[/b] and [b]Mod Settings[/b] are installed and working.[/*]
[*]Download the mod.[/*]
[*]Extract the contents into your [i]Subnautica2/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/[/i] folder.[/*]
[/list]
[size=4][color=#f9a85d][b]Configuration[/b][/color][/size]
The mod uses the [i]Mod Settings[/i] menu for in-game customization:
[list]
[*][b]Enabled Toggles:[/b] Toggle custom capacity for each locker type individually.[/*]
[*][b]Locker Settings:[/b] Adjust capacity by defining Rows and Columns (Total capacity = Rows * Columns).[/*]
[*][b]QuickBar Slots:[/b] Set the desired number of quickbar slots (1-10).[/*]
[/list]
[size=4][color=#f9a85d][b]Translators Welcome![/b][/color][/size]
Want to enjoy this mod in your native language? You can easily create your own translation!

[color=#f9a85d][b]How to translate:[/b][/color]
[list=1]
[*]Navigate to the [b][i]Translations[/i][/b] folder inside the mod directory.[/*]
[*]Make a copy of the [b][i]en.lua[/i][/b] file.[/*]
[*]Rename the copied file to your language's 2-letter code (e.g., [b][i]fr.lua[/i][/b] for French, [b][i]es.lua[/i][/b] for Spanish, [b][i]it.lua[/i][/b] for Italian).[/*]
[*]Open the file with any text editor and simply translate the text on the right side of the equal signs.[/*]
[/list]
[color=#f9a85d][b]Sharing your Translation:[/b][/color]
If you translated the mod, I would love to include it in the official files! Feel free to send me your translated [b][i].lua[/i][/b] file, and I will add it to the next update (with full credit to you, of course!).

If you prefer to upload your translation as a standalone mod/add-on on Nexus Mods, you are welcome to do so.
I only ask for two simple things:
[list]

[/list]

[color=#f9a85d][size=4][b]Troubleshooting & Installation Tips[/b][/size][/color]
[list]
[*][b]Folder Structure:[/b] Please ensure the files are extracted correctly. Your folder path should look like this:
[i]Subnautica2/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/CapacityQuickBarTweaks/Scripts/[/i]
If your zip extractor creates nested folders like [i]Scripts/Scripts/[/i], please move the files so they match the structure above.[/*]
[*][b]Mod Loading:[/b] This mod uses [i]enabled.txt[/i] to load automatically. If you have any issues, make sure the [i]enabled.txt[/i] is located directly inside the [i]CapacityQuickBarTweaks[/i] folder. If it still doesn't load, you can manually register it by adding [i]CapacityQuickBarTweaks : 1[/i] to your [i]ue4ss/Mods/mods.txt[/i] file.[/*]
[*][b]Language Changes:[/b] This mod automatically translates its settings based on your game language. However, changing your language in-game currently requires a [b]game restart[/b] for the newly translated texts to actually show up in the Mod Menu. [i](This is due to a current limitation of the Mod Settings framework).[/i] [/*]
[/list]
